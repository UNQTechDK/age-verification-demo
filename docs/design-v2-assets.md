# Storefront design v2

The UNQVerify logo in `public/unqverify-logo.png` is an unchanged copy of the existing brand asset from `age-verify-app/public/logo.png`.

`public/demo-catalog-v2.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP at quality 85. The three equal panels are displayed using CSS background positions in product cards and basket thumbnails. The existing hero photograph is retained.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create ONE photorealistic ecommerce catalog triptych image, landscape 1536x1024, divided into exactly three equal vertical panels with NO borders. Each panel has identical pale warm gray/off-white seamless studio background and generous negative space. Left third: ONE full silver aluminum beer can, minimal deep navy geometric curved label, no text, vertically centered with soft natural shadow. Middle third: ONE full elegant rounded amber aperitif glass bottle with natural oak stopper, no label, completely visible including top and base, vertically centered. Right third: ONE closed ivory round nicotine pouch tin, unbranded, slightly elevated three-quarter view so lid and front edge visible, vertically centered. Every object entirely contained within its own third, centered exactly at x 1/6, 1/2, 5/6. Product occupies about 55% of its panel width. Matched warm daylight, realistic materials, premium Scandinavian still-life catalog aesthetic. Each panel must work independently as a portrait product photo when cropped at the thirds. No extra objects, no decorations, no typography, no logos, no watermark. These are fictional demo products for NORDHANDEL ecommerce design.
